This notice covers this Tectra marketing site and messages sent using its contact links. It does not describe a future Tectra account or platform service.

Browsing this site

This site has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics scripts. Fonts, images and video are served with the site. The video plays only when you choose to play it. The motion preference changes the current page and is not saved to browser storage.

The service delivering a published site receives technical information such as your IP address and requested page to serve it. This is separate from the site code described above.

Contacting Cosmik

Contact links open your email application. No message is sent by this website. If you choose to email hello@cosmiklabs.org, Cosmik receives your address and the information you send and uses them to respond to your inquiry.

Cosmik’s company privacy notice explains its email handling. Contacting Cosmik does not subscribe you to a mailing list.
