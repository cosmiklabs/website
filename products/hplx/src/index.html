The creative loop

Make a change.
Feel the difference.

Move between making the scene and experiencing it. HPLX keeps the editor and playtest in the same place.

01 / Build

Give the room its character.

Place props, lights, sounds and script areas. Search the game’s assets and your story’s own files as you build.

02 / Playtest

Step into the scene.

Press F5 to play from the camera’s position. Test the route, the lighting and the moment you’re trying to create.

03 / Refine

Keep the idea moving.

Press Esc to return to editing. Adjust the scene and try it again—or pause play to tune a light while you see the result.

Building: the light and script area are part of the scene you create.

Before you start

A few things
to know.

Can I download HPLX yet?

Not yet. HPLX is in development, with no public release or announced release date. This page describes the work in progress.

Do I need to own Amnesia?

Yes. HPLX reads the maps, models, textures, audio and other content from your own local PC installation of Amnesia: The Dark Descent. No original game content is included with HPLX. A console copy does not provide the local PC files it needs.

Store-build compatibility is still being tested; owning a particular version is not a compatibility guarantee.

Will HPLX cost money?

HPLX will be free to use. Cosmik does not, and never will, require payment to use HPLX. The original game is separate and may cost money.

Is this an official Amnesia editor?

No. HPLX is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Frictional Games. Amnesia and its game content belong to their respective owners.

How does built-in playtesting work?

HPLX includes a reimplementation that reads the game files from your installation. That makes it possible to play a story inside the editor. Compatibility is still being developed and tested; built-in playtesting does not establish identical behavior to the original game.

The editor also provides a way to save and test a custom story in the original game.

What about other games and platforms?

The current focus is custom-story creation for the original Amnesia: The Dark Descent. Windows, Linux and Apple Silicon macOS packages are planned; there are no public packages yet. Tested requirements and limitations will accompany a release.

Will the source be available?

HPLX is intended to be open source under GPL-3.0-or-later. Its product source is not currently offered publicly. That license does not apply to the original game content or HPLX’s brand artwork.
